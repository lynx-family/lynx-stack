Render QR codes as high-resolution inline PNG images with a solid white background on terminals that support the Kitty Graphics Protocol (Ghostty, Kitty, WezTerm, Warp, Rio, Konsole) or iTerm2 Inline Images Protocol (iTerm2, Mintty), falling back to `uqr` compact Unicode rendering on unsupported terminals.
